ROBOTICS CAPSTONE / HARDWARE LEAD

SCAMPER Rover

A Raspberry Pi rover built to demonstrate hand gesture control, colour tracking, and ultrasonic obstacle avoidance.

My contribution. I designed the chassis in CAD, 3D printed the hardware, built the circuitry, and designed the custom PCB in KiCad.

How the hardware evolved

The team replaced the original LEGO chassis with a 3D-printed design. I designed a custom PCB to make the wiring more compact and reliable. It passed design checks and powered the Pi and sensors, but the motors failed to run during hardware testing. We used a solderable breadboard for the final build.
